# BarnesVidgen2002WebQual

An Integrative Approach to the Assessment of E-Commerce Quality
Stuart J. Barnes and Richard Vidgen (2002)
Journal of Electronic Commerce Research, Vol. 3, No. 3, pp. 114-127.

Abstract:
This paper reports on the development of WebQual 4.0, an instrument for assessing the quality of websites. WebQual 4.0 has three core dimensions:
1. Usability Quality: design of the website, ease of navigation, user-friendly interface, aesthetic appearance, appropriate multimedia, conveying competent image.
2. Information Quality: accurate information, believable information, timely and updated content, relevant information, easy to read and understand, right level of detail, appropriate format.
3. Service Interaction Quality: reputation of the organization, feeling safe in transactions and personal data privacy, providing clear feedback, space for community and communication, assurance that services are delivered as promised.

The WebQual 4.0 instrument utilizes a 22-item questionnaire evaluated using a 5-point or 7-point Likert scale to measure perceived website quality and user satisfaction.
