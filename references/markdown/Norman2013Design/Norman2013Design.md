# Norman2013Design

The Design of Everyday Things: Revised and Expanded Edition
Donald A. Norman (2013)
Basic Books, New York.

Fundamental Principles of Interaction Design:
1. Affordance: The relationship between the properties of an object and the capabilities of the agent that determine how the object could possibly be used.
2. Signifiers: Signals that communicate where the action should take place and what action is possible (e.g., buttons, persistent hotlines, call-to-action badges).
3. Constraints: Physical, logical, semantic, and cultural limitations that prevent incorrect actions.
4. Mapping: The relationship between controls and their movements or results in the world.
5. Feedback: Sending back information about what action has been done and what result has been accomplished, immediately and clearly.
6. Conceptual Model: A simplified explanation of how something works that enables mental simulation of system behavior.
7. Error Prevention and Recovery: Distinguishing slips from mistakes; designing tolerant systems that warn users and prevent destructive submissions.
