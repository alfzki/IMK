I. ABSTRACT:
As businesses extend their online offerings, demand
for UI-UX and digital services is increasing. Both
in-house teams and agencies will be under pressure to
offer services on time, with the total number of internet
users worldwide increasing by 301 million since April
2019 (We are Social). After the covid-19 it’s demand in
e-commerce market is also increasing exponentially. In
this paper User-centred design (UCD) method is used to
process the whole prototyping. User flow is also
discussed based on 3 levels(launch screen, create and
account, home screen). The test result for the sample
prototyping were the app's design elements with 4
points being the most common response on the scale of 5,
the main menu page and its experience with 5 points,
and the overall UI-UX of the app with 5 points.

Define-
Before the product team does anything, one of the
most crucial processes in UX design is completed.
You must first comprehend the context in which a
product exists before you can design it. The basis for
the final product is laid during the product definition

stakeholders brainstorm at the highest level about the
product(essentially, the productconcept).

Once you've outlined your concept, the product team
moves on to the research step. User and market
research are frequently included in this phase.
According to seasoned product designers, good
research drives design decisions, and doing research
earlyinthe processmay save a lotoftime and money
inthe longrun.
Because of the product's complexity, time, resources
available, and a number of other considerations, the
product research phase is possibly the most
changeableofallprojectphases.
Ideate-
The purpose of the analysis phase is to draw
inferences from the research findings, moving from
"what" people want/think/need to "why" they
want/think/need it. During this phase, designers
double-check that the team's most critical
assumptionsarevalid.
Design-
After determining what users want, need, and expect
from a product, product designers go on to the design
process. Product teams work on a variety of tasks at
this stage, ranging from information architecture (IA)
to UI design. A successful design process is both
highly collaborative (all team members participating
in product design must actively participate) and
iterative (meaning that it cycles back upon itself to
validate ideas).
Test-
Validationisacrucialpartofthe designprocesssince
it helps teams to see if their design is usable by their
target audience. Because end-user feedback is more
helpful when testing with high-fidelity designs, the
validation process normally begins after the
high-fidelity design is completed. During a series of
user testing sessions, the team verifies the product
withstakeholdersandend-users.

III. M ETHODOLOGY:
To finish this project, a UCD model was employed.
The preliminary review of requirements will be done
by querying and collecting user information
throughout the requirements formulation process.
The user design step follows, which entails the
creation of a prototype, testing, and refinement. If
necessary, this procedure is carried out depending on
user feedback. In the user design process, the process
is repeated until the refining results are satisfactory.
Following that, the proposeddesign will go through a
detailed building procedure. The proposed design can
then be applied by the user after it has been
constructed.
An improper interface design not also creates user
discomfort, but also impacts user awareness and
decreasesthedesiretouseit.[7]

Figure 4 shows the user flowfrom app start to closure
designwhichhas3levels ofasmentionedbelow:
A. Launch app: While your mobile app is loading,
splash screens (also known as start screens) give a
simple initial experience. They establish the stage for
your app while giving the app engine and your app
time toloadandinitialize.
B. Create an account: Signing up is the process of
creating anew account. While differentonline portals
may use different names for returning users, they
uniformly use the term "sign up" to describe the act
of registering for the first time. It simply implies
creating a new account, whether it's for a portal, an
application,oranewsletter.
C. Home screen: Any application's home screen is a
must-have feature. It is the main screen of a mobile
app from which users interact with most of the
program's options. Although the design of home
screens varies based on the type of product and its
intended use, there are several key aspects that are
presentinallofthem.
