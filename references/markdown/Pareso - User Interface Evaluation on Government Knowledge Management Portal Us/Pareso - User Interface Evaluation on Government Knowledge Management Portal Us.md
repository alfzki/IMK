Abstract—Currently, the society is very critical and demands 
valid information transparency. Information transparency is 
needed to maintain good governance and can also prevent 
corruption. However, information on the sustainability of 
development programs carried out by the government is much 
unknown to the public. In addition, the government must be 
burdened with public dissatisfaction due to fake news (hoax). 
The Ministry of Public Works has tried to build a website to 
disseminate its programs. However, measurement of the quality 
of the website is needed. This study uses questionnaires with a 
Likert scale to obtain user interface assessment data in 
accordance with the WebQual 4.0 method. This study shows 
that the easiness of the website to use has the highest correlation 
with how helpful the website is. Public works ministry websites 
need to more frequently update valid information and promote 
transparency. The society also wants online complaint platform 
to make it easier to solve problems. 
Keywords—evaluation, user interface, government portal, 
WebQual 
I. INTRODUCTION 
The current state of information technology, especially the 
internet has changed many human activities. Many internet-
based applications are commonly used by the public such as 
e-mail, social media, and websites. Website development for 
an institution is something that is very much needed. Along 
with these developments, the Ministry of Public Works needs 
to convey information about tasks, functions and all its 
activities to the public through the internet media (PU-net) in 
a new, more structured view. This research is needed for the 
development of PU-net that is in accordance with the user's 
perception in meeting the needs for valid information of the 
Ministry of Public Works program.  
This Information System is demanded to improve the 
performance of government institutions by providing services 
that meet their needs. An online system is implemented by the 
Ministry of Public Works to improve performance and 
effectiveness. With evolving technology, the government 
institution is expected to provide maximum service quality. 
Information that is accurate, complete, adequate, reliable, and 
accepted by the intended recipient is termed of information 
quality. Continuity of information systems depends on the 
quality of the system that affects user satisfaction [1]. 
Identification and selection of information cases related to the 
current trend using the sampling method. The ideas proposed 
will be combined with strategies in related studies [2]. 
The discussion of various approaches to extracting 
temporal information by using research on taking temporal 
information was studied by Campos. Practices associated with 
structure, representation, access, and data warehouse 
information are called information retrieval [3]. To manage 
data or knowledge that satisfies the user's needs is the purpose 
of information retrieval [4]. Information is generated using 
information technology with precision calculations to support 
managerial roles in the decision-making process [5]. 
Website evaluation uses various methods with multiple 
criteria which are usually based on the user's perception [6]. 
Currently, only WebQual has a measurement instrument that 
focuses on user perceptions of the quality of the website [7]. 
WebQual is a development method of SERVQUAL that can 
be used to measure the quality of a website [8].Theory of 
Theory of Reasoned Action (TRA) originating from social 
psychology explains how the influence of attitude on 
behavior. TRA consists of three constructs, namely behavioral 
intention, attitude, and subjective norm [9].  Technology 
Acceptance Model (TAM) is a model for measuring user 
acceptance of technology that employs variables Perceived 
Ease of Use, Perceived Usefulness, and Intention to Use [10]. 
UTAUT measures the influence of performance expectancy, 
effort expectancy, and social influence on the behavioral 
intention to use technology. While behavioral intention affects 
technology use [11]. E-S-Qual measures quality based on 
efficiency, fulfillment, system availability, and privacy [12].

information through internet media, books, e-books, and

selection is done by simplified random sampling. 
Questionnaires are administered to the web user using a Likert 
scale: (1) is strongly agree, (2) is agree, (3) is disagree, (4) is 
strongly disagree.

3) Webqual 4.0: Webqual performs measurement 
techniques based on the perception of the end user. Webqual 
is a method that has been developed from Servqual compiled 
by Parasuraman[13]. The WebQual method uses the approach 
of perception and importance of the user[14]. Useful 
perception of a good information system is a system where 
users are satisfied with the quality of the website. This quality 
is contained in 12 dimensions of WebQual [15][16][17]: 
• Informational Fit-To-Task 
• Tailored Information 
• Trust 
• Response Time 
• Ease of Understanding 
• Intuitive Operations 
• Visual Appeal 
• Innovativeness 
• Emotional Appeal 
• Consistent Image 
• On-Line Completeness 
• Relative Advantage 
From those variables, including the usefulness of the 
website, the authors made some adjustment and constructed 7 
questions. The questions were asked to find the user point of 
view toward the ministry of public works website. Those 
questions are:  
• The visual appearance of the UI  
• The easiness of the UI  
• The easiness of the content to understand  
• The trust in the content  
• The trust in the privacy  
• The admin responds toward question or discussion in 
the forum  
• And the usefulness/helpfulness of the website  
  
Beside those question, the authors added few more 
questions about the government and about website in general 
to measure the respondent interest in the website content.  
The result is analysed with correlation method. The 
variable with the highest correlation with the 
usefulness/helpfulness of the website is the variable that has 
the largest influence in user point of view. On the other hand, 
the variable with lowest correlation is the variable that has the 
smallest influence.

IV. CONCLUSSION 
Based on a survey which was conducted using WebQual 
4.0 on young and educated population, we have extracted 
information from 70 respondent. We used questionnaire 
which consist of 16 questions. The questions can be divided 
into 3 section. The study chose Webqual 4.0 compared to 
other methods because only Webqual 4.0 has a measurement 
instrument that focuses on user perception of the quality of a 
website. 
In the first section, the questions related to government and 
national development in general. The respondents show high 
interest to keep up with national development news, and the 
majority of them want the national development to be 
transparent to public. They also want an online platform for 
reporting complaints since some of them does not satisfied 
with government respond toward their complaints. 
In the second section, website user interface in general, 
almost all the respondents think that user interface is 
important. However, some of them also said that, there are 
other factors that are more important. One factor that we 
mentioned on the questionnaire is the website integrity which 
respondents are very disturbed with. 
In the last section, minister of  works website, the result 
shows that the easiness of the website to use has the highest 
correlation with how helpful the website is. Meanwhile the 
visual appearance of the website shows lowest correlation. 
According to the results of this study, we suggest to make 
the minister of works website as easy as possible to use and 
make the content of the website easy to understand to improve 
the minister of works website helpfulness.
