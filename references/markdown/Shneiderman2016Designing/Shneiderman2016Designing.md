# Shneiderman2016Designing

Designing the User Interface: Strategies for Effective Human-Computer Interaction (6th Edition)
Ben Shneiderman, Catherine Plaisant, Maxine S. Cohen, Steven Jacobs, Niklas Elmqvist, and Nicholas Diakopoulos (2016)
Pearson, Boston.

Key Strategies for Emergency Systems and Life-Critical User Interfaces:
1. Rapid Information Access: In life-critical emergency systems (such as fire dispatch, 911/113, and disaster response), users face severe cognitive stress and panic. Crucial emergency hotline numbers must be placed prominently above the fold without requiring multi-level menu navigation.
2. Eight Golden Rules of Interface Design:
   - Strive for consistency across layouts, colors, and fonts.
   - Seek universal usability accommodating diverse citizen capabilities.
   - Offer informative feedback for every user action.
   - Design dialogs to yield closure, giving relief and confirmation.
   - Prevent errors through form validation and input masks.
   - Permit easy reversal of actions.
   - Keep users in control.
   - Reduce short-term memory load by presenting information concisely.
3. Time-Critical Response: Reducing clicks and keystrokes directly improves emergency response effectiveness.
